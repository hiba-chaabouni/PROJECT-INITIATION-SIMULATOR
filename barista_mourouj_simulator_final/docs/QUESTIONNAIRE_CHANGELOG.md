# Questionnaire → Simulator Change Log

This version implements the supplied questionnaire answers for Sections E–K and updates the simulator accordingly.

## E — Project Scope

- Mandatory core scope is fixed.
- Optional scope is now recorded as four explicit Yes/No choices:
  - Hot Kitchen
  - Outdoor Terrace
  - Glovo Delivery Setup
  - Grand Launch Campaign
- The charter generates the exact in-scope wording specified in the questionnaire.
- Unselected optional components are automatically listed outside scope.
- Mandatory and optional opening capabilities are reflected in the charter.

## F — Constraints & Trade-offs

- Budget ceiling: 650,000 TND.
- Maximum tolerance: 715,000 TND.
- Target opening: 01 July 2027.
- Maximum acceptable delay: 29 July 2027.
- Constraint priority remains a three-option decision.
- Inconsistency warnings are surfaced in the charter comments.

## G — Deliverables

The charter distinguishes mandatory deliverables from optional deliverables selected through the scope decision.

## H — Success Criteria

The final evaluation now reports individual criteria as:

- Met
- At Risk
- Not Met

Each criterion includes contextual feedback describing which decisions influenced the result.

## I — Project Approach

The approach choices and their stated qualitative/quantitative effects follow the questionnaire:

- Internal-led
- Outsourced fit-out
- Mixed / hybrid

The selected approach is recorded in the charter together with stakeholder needs, assumptions and constraints.

## J — Risk Model

The simulator carries the six initial risks, uses Low / Medium / High probability and impact categories, and reports the top three risks in the charter.

An active High Probability + High Impact risk contributes to the AT RISK condition.

## K — Project Charter

The former card-based charter was replaced with a compact professional document structure:

1. Header / identity
2. Baseline fields
3. Project overview
4. Scope
5. Approach and constraints
6. Tentative schedule
7. Major risks
8. Roles and responsibilities
9. Comments / notes
10. Formal authorization and signatures

The layout follows the uploaded reference's structural idea — header fields, overview table, scope table and milestone table — while retaining the questionnaire and Chapter 3 content.
