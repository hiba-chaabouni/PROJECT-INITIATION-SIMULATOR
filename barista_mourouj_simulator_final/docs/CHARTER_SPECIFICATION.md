# Project Charter — Content Specification

This document defines the content model used by the simulator's Project Charter. The visual layout is inspired by compact professional one-page charter templates, but the content follows the project's questionnaire answers and Chapter 3 rather than copying the reference template's labels.

## 1. Header / identity

- Project title: Barista Mourouj Branch Opening Project
- Authorization date: 04 January 2027
- Project Manager: selected in D2
- Project Sponsor: selected in D1
- PM contact: role-based classroom placeholder; no real individual contact information
- Organizational unit: Barista Mourouj Project Team / simulation
- Planned start: 04 January 2027
- Calculated completion: derived from the target opening date plus/minus simulation effects
- Estimated cost: calculated from the selected business-case option, project structure, scope, approach and constraint priority
- Budget ceiling: 650,000 TND; tolerance threshold 715,000 TND

## 2. Project overview

### Problem / opportunity

The scenario concerns the opportunity to establish a fictional Barista branch in Mourouj while balancing customer opportunity, brand consistency, stakeholder needs and initiation constraints.

### Purpose / business need

Open one Mourouj branch on time, within the approved budget limits, and to the required Barista-standard quality.

### Business case

The selected business-case recommendation and secondary objective are carried forward from the class decisions. Financial figures are explicitly treated as simulation assumptions.

### Objectives and success metrics

- Open by 01 July 2027; maximum acceptable delay: 29 July 2027.
- Total spend target: 650,000 TND; maximum tolerance: 715,000 TND.
- Brand compliance target: at least 90%.
- Obtain all mandatory municipal, health and hygiene permits.
- Meet professional governance expectations and sponsor approval.

## 3. Deliverables

### Mandatory

- Fully fitted-out retail branch venue
- Recruited and trained store staff
- Installed commercial equipment and operational menu
- Municipal, hygiene and operating permits

### Optional — selected through D7

- Operational Hot Kitchen facility
- Outdoor Terrace seating space
- Integrated Glovo delivery channel
- Executed Grand Launch marketing campaign

## 4. Scope

### Within scope

Generated as:

> Opening of one Barista's branch in Mourouj including [selected scope components].

### Outside scope

- Renovations or modifications to other existing Barista locations
- Corporate logo redesign or brand overhaul
- Construction of a coffee roasting facility
- Custom mobile app development
- Any unselected optional scope component

### Mandatory opening capabilities

- Espresso and beverage service
- Indoor seating area
- Customer Wi-Fi
- Digital POS / payment processing
- Health and hygiene compliance

## 5. Approach

The charter records the selected approach in the questionnaire's required form:

> Approach: [Selected Approach Option]. Stakeholder needs: [Key Needs Summary]. Assumptions: [Key Assumptions]. Constraints: [Key Constraints].

Approach options:

- Internal-led
- Outsourced fit-out
- Mixed / hybrid

## 6. Schedule

The simulator generates a compact milestone view for the charter. Milestone dates are illustrative planning assumptions derived from the fictional start/finish constraints; they are not Barista schedules.

## 7. Major risks

The charter displays the top three risks extracted for the project. The full risk set remains represented in the simulator's risk/business-case logic.

Initial risks:

- R01 Competitor Retention
- R02 Quality Inconsistency
- R03 Fit-out Delays
- R04 Cost Overrun
- R05 Hiring Shortfall
- R06 Permit Delays

Probability and impact use Low / Medium / High categories. An active High Probability + High Impact risk triggers an AT RISK condition.

## 8. Roles and responsibilities

The charter uses role-based entries rather than real individual names or contact information.

The sponsor, Project Manager, project team and key stakeholders are represented with responsibility and signature fields.

## 9. Comments / warnings

Comments are generated from unresolved inconsistency conditions, including:

- Full optional scope + budget priority
- Kitchen + terrace + narrow schedule buffer
- External PM + outsourced fit-out without explicit internal brand oversight
- Estimated cost above the 715,000 TND tolerance
- Calculated completion beyond the 4-week schedule tolerance

## 10. Formal authorization

The charter uses the Chapter 3 authorization meaning:

> By signing this document, key stakeholders formally recognize the project and grant the Project Manager the authority to apply organizational resources to project activities.

Signature placeholders are provided for the Sponsor, Project Manager and a key stakeholder.
